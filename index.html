Why CSE + materials science

Where the two fields overlap

Materials research produces data — XRD scans, mechanical test cycles, degradation curves. My CSE background lets me process it: writing the models that turn raw data into a process recommendation. The minor teaches me what to measure; the major gives me the tools to build systems that measure and model it.
