Why CSE + materials science

Where the two fields overlap

Materials research produces data: XRD scans, mechanical test cycles, degradation curves. My CSE background is what lets me process that data — writing the Python/R models that turn thousands of data points into a process recommendation, and building the automation and embedded systems that could run these experiments directly. The minor gives me the materials background to know what to measure; the major gives me the tools to build the systems that measure and model it.
